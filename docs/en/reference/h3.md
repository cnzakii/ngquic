# HTTP/3

`ngquic.h3` speaks HTTP/3 ([RFC 9114](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9114))
and QPACK ([RFC 9204](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9204)) over a QUIC
connection. Pass `h3=H3Config()` to `Endpoint.connect()` or `Incoming.accept()`
to get an `H3Connection`; it inherits the connection lifecycle, timers and
paths from [`BaseConnection`](connections.md#ngquic.BaseConnection).
